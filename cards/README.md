# Tarot card images

Rider–Waite–Smith tarot, art by Pamela Colman Smith, first published 1909 (William Rider & Son).
Public domain: in the US because it was published before 1930, and in the UK/EU because the artist died in 1951.

Source: scans of an early printing on Wikimedia Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite_tarot_deck),
taken from https://github.com/seidkonatarot/tarot-cards and resized to 300 px wide WebP.

These are NOT the 1971 U.S. Games recoloured edition, which is under separate copyright.

File names: m00–m21 major arcana; w = wands, c = cups, s = swords, p = pentacles;
01 ace … 10, 11 page, 12 knight, 13 queen, 14 king.
